## Acceptance coverage implemented

- Explicit subtree drops cover script, style, template, SVG, MathML, iframe/object/embed, and specified form controls.
- Comments and parser instructions/doctypes are excluded from the renderer-facing tree.
- Unsupported/custom elements are transparent wrappers: safe children are preserved in source order while raw tag names/attributes are not retained.
- Header, footer, nav, article and other structural elements needed by later renderers are preserved.
- Retained attributes are allowlisted per element. Event handlers, style, srcset, data-* and unrelated attributes do not reach writers.
- Entity decoding is not repeated; the cleaner consumes parser-decoded text as-is.
- Traversal is iterative rather than recursively walking attacker-controlled depth.
