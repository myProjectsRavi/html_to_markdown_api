## Verified platform snapshot

Dated 2026-10-07 official documentation was used:

- Cloudflare Workers Free: 100,000 requests/day per account, 10 ms CPU/invocation, 128 MB memory.
- RapidAPI: request quotas may be daily or monthly, hard limits can stop calls at the quota, and per-plan rate limits support second/minute/hour timeframes.
- RapidAPI documented default quota behavior excludes 5xx responses from usage increments; therefore the model does not treat non-5xx rejection traffic as free.

References:
- https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/

## Capacity model

Machine-readable worksheet: `docs/capacity/free-account-envelope.json`.

The model treats the Cloudflare allowance as account-wide and separately accounts for:

- other Workers;
- staging;
- health checks;
- expected customers;
- rejected/direct-origin traffic;
- a separate safety reserve.

The candidate API reserve is 20,000 requests/day and the safety reserve is 10,000/day. They are explicit planning placeholders, not proof of available capacity and not marketplace configuration.

Four monthly stress scenarios are converted into both 30-day average demand and the conservative one-day burst where the entire monthly allowance can arrive in one day without a tighter rate limit.
